Why Tiru exists

The problem is often not the care. It is the gap around it.

Years inside healthcare exposed the same barriers repeatedly: people cannot find reliable information, providers struggle to prove what is happening, and important operational work still lives in paper, calls and scattered messages.

01 · DISCOVERY

Information gaps

Which facility is open? Does it offer the service? Where is it? Can I pay there? People should not need six phone calls to find out.

Verification gaps

Healthcare decisions deserve information that is current, provider-owned and checked before it is presented as official.

Our story

From clinical practice to digital infrastructure.

“I chose digital health because improving one system can improve the lives of thousands of patients.”Dr. Anteneh Tirusew · Founder

Tiru grew from practical problems encountered in healthcare: finding facilities, coordinating clinicians, understanding operations and making digital tools work in the realities of an Ethiopian health system.

CLINICAL
Inside the system

Medical practice across outpatient, emergency, inpatient and night-duty settings shaped the problems worth solving.

DIGITAL
First-hand digital health

Healthcare operations, teleconsultation and digital workflows exposed the opportunity to improve systems beyond a single patient encounter.

FIRST TOOLS
Small systems, real problems

Internal tools for physician scheduling and healthcare referral mapping became the seeds of a broader platform.

2026
Tiru Health

Tiru Medical Directory and Tiru Ops now sit under one brand and one standard: trusted infrastructure for healthcare.
