Why Tiru exists

Healthcare rarely fails for lack of expertise. It fails in the gaps between people, information and systems.

Years of clinical practice surfaced the same three barriers again and again. Tiru was built to remove them.

Information barriers

Nobody should have to phone six people to learn which clinic is open, what it costs, or whether the service they need is offered there at all.

Answered by the Directory

Trust barriers

A healthcare decision deserves verified information — licensed facilities, confirmed by the providers themselves and checked before it is published.

Answered by verification

Accountability barriers

Facilities are asked to prove coverage, staffing and compliance — often with paper registers that were never designed to answer the question.

Answered by Tiru Ops

Tiru Ops

A fingerprint scanner tells you when someone arrived and left. Tiru Ops tells you where they were throughout the day — and turns that into records a facility can stand behind.

  • GPS-geofenced presence verification — no hardware to buy, install or maintain
  • Real-time visibility from anywhere: a live shift board across every department
  • Multiple centres under one control panel, from a single login
  • Daily, weekly and monthly attendance flagging, exportable for payroll and audit
  • Digital leave requests routed to leadership for approval
  • Incident reporting with a documented, reviewable trail
  • One message from leadership to every member of staff, instantly
  • Kiosk check-in so cleaning and security teams are never left out

Inside the platforms

What each one actually does.

Every capability below started as a problem someone described on a ward, at a reception desk, or on a phone call that should never have been necessary.

Discovery

Search that understands care

Search by service, facility type, specialist or location — and see what is nearest to you first, sorted by real distance.

Detail

What you need before you travel

Services, hours, payment methods, walk-in or appointment policy, contact numbers and check-up packages, on one page.

Verification

Licence-gated approval

Operating and business licences, with issue and expiry dates, are required before a facility can carry the Official badge.

Ownership

Provider console

Facilities claim their listing and edit it live — services, specialists, photographs and hours stay current because the provider keeps them current.

Specialists

Find a named doctor

Search specialists directly and see where they practise, what they treat and whether an appointment is required.

Correction

Report what is wrong

Anyone can submit a correction. Each one is reviewed by a person before it changes what the public sees.

Presence

Verified check-in

Staff check in inside a geofence drawn around the facility, with QR and kiosk fallbacks. Records hold even when the network does not.

Compliance

Defensible hour records

Weekly presence assembled automatically into records a facility can present to a regulator, an owner or a board without reconstructing anything by hand.

Coverage

Live shift board

Every department, every shift, who is on and who is missing — visible now rather than at the end of the month.

Communication

Direct broadcast

Leadership reaches every member of staff at once, including teams no memo has ever reached, with no intermediary in between.

Workflow

Leave and incidents

Leave requests and incident reports move through defined approval paths, leaving a trail instead of a group chat.

Equity

Kiosk access

A PIN terminal for staff without smartphones. Cleaning, security and support teams are part of the system from day one — not a later phase.

Our story

From clinical practice to digital infrastructure.

Tiru did not begin as a company. It began as two small tools built to fix problems that kept getting in the way of care.

I chose digital health because improving one system can improve the lives of thousands of patients. Dr. Anteneh Tirusew · Founder

Five years in private practice — outpatient, emergency, inpatient and night duty — followed by physician coordination, healthcare operations, and work on one of Ethiopia's early EMR-integrated telemedicine platforms. Tiru is the same commitment to healthcare, applied at the level of systems rather than single encounters.

2021
Clinical practice

Medical training completed; years of private practice across outpatient, emergency, inpatient and night-duty care.

2022
Digital health, first-hand

Operations management and teleconsultation on an EMR-integrated telemedicine platform — including a women's health programme reaching underserved communities.

First tools
Two small internal systems

A physician duty board that generated shift schedules automatically, and a referral map of private facilities across Addis Ababa.

Fellowship
Problems validated properly

Through the Africa Healthcare Innovation Fellowship: testing the problems against other innovators before scaling any solution.

2026
Tiru Health

Two internal tools become two platforms — Tiru Medical Directory and Tiru Ops — under one brand and one standard.
