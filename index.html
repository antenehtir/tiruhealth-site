Why Tiru exists

Healthcare rarely fails for lack of expertise. It fails in the gaps.

Years at the bedside surfaced the same three gaps again and again — between people, information and systems. Tiru was built to close them.

01

Finding care

No one should need six phone calls to learn which clinic is open, what it offers, or how to get there.

→ Tiru Medical Directory
02

Trusting information

A health decision deserves information kept current by the providers themselves — and open to correction.

→ Verified, facility-managed listings
03

Proving delivery

Facilities must prove coverage and staff presence, often with paper registers never built for the question.

→ Tiru Ops

Operations intelligence · for facilities

Tiru Ops

A fingerprint tells you when someone arrived. Tiru Ops tells you where they were all day.

  • Verified on-site presence — QR plus GPS geofence, no hardware to buy
  • A live view of who is on shift, late or missing — from anywhere
  • Audit-ready hour records, leave, incidents and direct staff notices
  • A kiosk so staff without smartphones are never left out

Our story
“I chose digital health because improving one system can improve the lives of thousands of patients.”
AT
Dr. Anteneh TirusewFounder · General Practitioner

Tiru did not begin as a company. It began as two small tools, built to fix problems that kept getting in the way of care — a duty board that planned physician shifts, and a referral map of private facilities across Addis Ababa.

“Tiru” means good in Amharic — a byword for quality. It is the standard we hold every product to.

  1. Clinical practice

    Five years at the bedside

    Outpatient, emergency, inpatient and night duty in private facilities.

  2. Digital health

    Operations and telemedicine

    Physician coordination and teleconsultation on an EMR-integrated platform.

  3. Fellowship

    Problems validated properly

    Africa Healthcare Innovation Fellowship, 2026.

  4. Today

    Tiru Health

    Two live platforms under one brand and one standard.
